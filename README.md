# Ra-Tod-ed Drag Racing

by Ra Tod

Ra Tod's racing game, in two modes. DRAG: the Twin Strip Showdown — two
950 m strips with a collapsing tunnel, a river jump, and a hill → bridge →
launch ramp on the way home; split times for each strip plus a total.
DESTRUCTO: a big open arena ringed by a barrier wall, with bleachers and
crowds on a centre island — grab orbs, gain abilities, and be the last
vehicle rolling (lives are selectable, or play a timed bout on
eliminations).

The cast is Ra's: the Short Bus, the Fetty Bus, Ted's Grey Truck, Lil
Tone's Sprint Car, Bobby's BP Cruiser, and ACEMAN's Monster Buggy. Pick
your vehicle — your three opponents are picked for you. Burnouts, visible damage, engines that can blow, real throttle
control (keyboard, touch, or gamepad), a settings menu with cheats,
weather, and the RA TOD boot splash + bus-launch intro video up front.
No music — racing sounds only.

Built on Kart Royale by Ryan Campbell (MIT) — see LICENSE. Unlisted preview build.
